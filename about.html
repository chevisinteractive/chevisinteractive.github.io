About the studio

A small studio. You talk to the builder.

Chevis Interactive is a small studio based in Norman, Oklahoma. It builds websites for local businesses across the Oklahoma City metro, and games and apps for Android played around the world. There is no account manager and no hand-off: when you hire the studio, the person you talk to is the person building your site.

The craft, in practical terms

Finished means finished

A site is not done when it loads. It is done when your customers can read it on a phone in bright sunlight, tap your number without zooming, and understand your offer in one pass. That is the bar every page is checked against before launch.

Honest by default

Fixed prices in the open. No dark patterns, no invented testimonials, no claims we cannot back up. If a smaller job will do, we say so, even when a bigger one would pay us more.

Your stuff stays yours

Your domain, your website, your files. Registered in your name and handed over in full if you ever leave. A studio you cannot leave is not a service, it is a trap, and we do not build traps.

Where we work

Home base is Norman, Oklahoma. Most website clients are local businesses across the Oklahoma City metro — close enough that a face-to-face conversation is always possible. The games side of the studio ships worldwide on Google Play.

How to reach the builder

Directly. Email chevisinteractive@gmail.com, or use the project inquiry form. Every message is read by the person who would build your site, and answered within one business day.
