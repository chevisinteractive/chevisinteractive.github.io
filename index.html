Questions

Before you ask

How much does a website cost, all in?
The build is $2,500 one time. After that, the optional Care Plan is $199/month and covers hosting management, maintenance, and small changes. Your domain name renews at cost, in your name. Anything beyond that is quoted and approved by you before work starts. There are no other charges hiding anywhere.
Do I have to take the Care Plan?
No. It is optional. Some owners want a website person on call; some would rather take the keys and drive. Either way the site and domain are yours, and you can start Care later if you change your mind.
What counts as a "small change" on the Care Plan?
Routine updates: text and photo swaps, hours, contact details, service and price updates, seasonal notices. Bigger work, like a new page, a booking system, or a redesign, is quoted separately at a fair price before we start. The full Care Plan, defined →
I already have a website. Can you fix it instead?
Often, yes. We will look at what you have and tell you honestly whether a refresh will do the job or a rebuild is worth it. If a refresh will do, we will say so.
Do I own my website and domain?
Yes. Both are registered in your name. If you ever move on, everything goes with you, with no exit fee and no hard feelings.
