The Craft

How we build

01

Polish is the product

Motion, typography, and the feel of every tap are the feature — not the garnish. If a screen doesn't feel finished, it isn't.

02

Honest by default

No dark patterns, no bought reviews, no pay-to-win. Free means free, a price means one price, and our listings only claim what the game actually does.

03

Your stuff stays yours

Tome plays files from your Drive and keeps no copies. Our apps collect the minimum, say exactly what it is, and let you delete it.
