A front desk for your website that never closes

The AI Setup puts an assistant on your website, trained on your business. It answers the questions you get asked every day — hours, services, prices, where to park — and when a visitor is ready, it captures their inquiry and sends it straight to you. Even at 10 PM. Even on Sunday.

What is included for $299

  • The assistant set up and configured for your business
  • Trained on your hours, services, prices, and common questions
  • Installed on your website, styled to match it

What it is, and what it isn't

It answers routine questions from the information you give it, and hands everything else to you. It does not invent prices, promise appointments it cannot see, or pretend to be a person — visitors can always reach you directly instead.

The assistant runs on a third-party service. If that service charges a subscription for your level of use, it is billed directly to you at cost, and we quote the exact figure before we start. No markup, no surprises.
