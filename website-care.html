Included every month

  • Hosting managed for you, on your own domain
  • Monitoring, so we know if your site goes down before your customers do
  • Regular backups, kept ready to restore
  • Security updates applied for you
  • Small changes done for you (defined below)
  • A short monthly report: what we did, what we checked, anything you should know
  • Direct support from the people who built your site

What counts as a small change

  • Text updates: wording, announcements, seasonal notices
  • Photo swaps and new photos added
  • Hours, contact details, and address updates
  • Service and price updates on existing pages
  • Most small changes land within two business days of your message

Bigger work — a new page, a booking system, an online store, a redesign — is quoted separately at a fair price and approved by you before we start. It is never billed as a surprise.

The terms, in plain words

Is the Care Plan required?
No. It is optional. Your website and domain are yours whether or not you take it. Some owners prefer to handle updates themselves; the plan exists for owners who would rather never think about it.
How is it billed?
$199 per month, billed monthly. There is no minimum term and no annual contract.
What happens if I cancel?
You tell us, and it stops at the end of the month you have paid for. Your website and domain go with you, and we will hand over everything needed to run the site elsewhere. No exit fee, no hostage situation.
Are hosting and domain costs included?
Hosting is included while you are on the Care Plan. Your domain name is registered in your name and renews at cost, so it is always truly yours; we will tell you the exact renewal figure before it comes due.
What if I need a big change?
We quote it separately, in writing, before any work starts. You approve the price or you don't, and nothing moves until you do.
How fast do changes land?
Most small changes are done within two business days of your message. If something is urgent, tell us in the message and we will treat it that way.
