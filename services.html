Questions

Fair questions

How long does a website take?
A small business site is usually ready for your review within a few weeks of kickoff. The pace usually depends on content: photos, text, and details from your side. We will give you a realistic timeline before we start, and we will tell you early if anything changes.
What does the Care Plan cover?
Hosting management, keeping the site backed up and up to date, small edits such as hours, prices, photos, and announcements, a short monthly report, and answers from us when you have questions. Bigger changes, like a new page or a redesign, are agreed with you before any work starts.
Can you update my existing site instead?
Often, yes. Send us the address and we will take a proper look. If a refresh will do the job, we will say so. If the site needs rebuilding to be fast and easy to run, we will say that too, and explain why.
Who owns the website and the domain?
You do. The domain is registered in your name, and the website is your property. If you ever move on, everything goes with you. We think that is the only fair way to do it.
How do we start?
Email chevisinteractive@gmail.com with a line or two about your business and your current site, if you have one. We will reply with a few questions and a clear next step. No pressure, and no obligation.
