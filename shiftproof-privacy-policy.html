ShiftProof Privacy Policy

Publisher: Chevis Interactive
App: ShiftProof (Android)
Effective date: November 12, 2026
Contact: chevisinteractive@gmail.com

ShiftProof is published by Chevis Interactive. This policy explains what information ShiftProof handles, where that information stays, and the choices you have.

The short version

ShiftProof does not require an account. Chevis Interactive does not operate a ShiftProof server, does not receive your shifts or pay records, and does not include advertising or analytics trackers in ShiftProof.

The jobs, shifts, pay rules, and paycheck figures you enter are stored in the app on your device so ShiftProof can calculate expected pay and compare it with a paycheck.

Information stored by the app on your device

When you use ShiftProof, the app stores the information needed to provide its features, which may include:

  • job names or labels you create;
  • hourly rates and pay rules;
  • overtime, differential, holiday, break, tip, bonus, and estimated-deduction settings;
  • shift start and end times, including a running shift;
  • pay-period and payday settings;
  • paycheck amounts you enter and the app's comparison results;
  • notes you add to a shift;
  • reminder and display preferences;
  • home-screen widget state; and
  • trial and unlock status.

This information is stored in ShiftProof's app-private local storage on your device.

How the information is used

ShiftProof uses the information locally to:

  • record and display your shifts;
  • calculate hours, expected gross, and estimated net pay;
  • apply the pay rules you configure;
  • compare an expected amount with a paycheck amount you enter;
  • show reports and trends;
  • provide the home-screen widget;
  • schedule local reminders; and
  • create reports and backup files when you ask it to.

Chevis Interactive does not use your shift or pay records for advertising, profiling, or sale.

What Chevis Interactive receives

Chevis Interactive does not receive your jobs, shifts, rates, notes, paycheck amounts, reports, or backup files from the app.

ShiftProof contains no Chevis analytics service and no advertising SDK. Google Play may provide developers with aggregate app-install, store, crash, and purchase reporting through Play Console under Google's own terms and privacy policy. That reporting is not a feed of your ShiftProof records.

Purchases

The optional full unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells ShiftProof whether the unlock is owned so the app can enable it.

Chevis Interactive does not receive or store your payment-card details. Google's handling of payment and Play account information is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Sharing and exports you control

ShiftProof does not automatically share your records.

The app lets you create and share PDF reports, CSV files, and a JSON backup through Android's share sheet or file tools. If you send one of those files to another person or service, that recipient will be able to read the information in the file. You control whether to create a file, where to send it, and whether to delete it.

Importing a ShiftProof backup replaces the app's current jobs, rules, shifts, holidays, and comparison records with the contents of the file you selected. Only import a backup you trust.

Android device backup

ShiftProof permits Android app backup. If you enable Android's device or app backup on your device, some ShiftProof local data may be included in that backup under your Google account and device settings. Chevis Interactive does not operate or receive that backup.

You can manage Android backup through your device and Google account settings. Clearing ShiftProof's app storage or uninstalling the app removes the copy stored by the app on that device, subject to any separate backup copy you control.

Permissions

ShiftProof may request or use:

  • Notifications, if you allow them, for payday and long-shift reminders;
  • Boot completed, so local reminders can be restored after the device restarts; and
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback.

ShiftProof does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, call logs, text messages, or health data.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your records remain in ShiftProof until you edit or delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Because Chevis Interactive does not hold a server copy of your ShiftProof records, Chevis Interactive cannot retrieve or remotely delete the records stored on your device. Exported reports and backups remain wherever you saved or sent them and can be deleted there.

Security

ShiftProof stores its working data in app-private storage provided by Android. The app does not transmit your shift and pay records to Chevis Interactive. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep your Android backup and exported files secure, because those records can contain sensitive employment and pay information.

Children

ShiftProof is intended for adults who work for pay. It is not directed to children.

Estimates

ShiftProof calculates from the information and rules you enter. Its results are estimates and records for your own review; they are not a payroll, tax, accounting, or legal determination.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes to how ShiftProof handles records will be reflected in the app and its Play Store disclosures.
