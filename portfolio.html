Real · Case study

Chevis Interactive — our own site

The problem

The studio's original site led with its games and mentioned website services near the bottom, with no prices anywhere. A business owner landing on it could not tell what we sell, what it costs, or how to start — the three things a services site exists to answer.

The decisions

Lead with the service and the price. Put all three prices in the open with their inclusions. Replace the email-only contact path with a one-minute inquiry form. Move the games to their own page, where fans get a better page and owners stop tripping over them.

What was built

A services-led homepage, dedicated pages for the build, the Care Plan, and the AI Setup add-on, a portfolio that shows work in desktop and phone frames, and plain-words terms for pricing, cancellation, and ownership.

What it demonstrates

Mobile-first layout, honest pricing presentation, a working lead-capture form wired to the studio inbox, and copy written around what the customer gets. You are looking at the deliverable, not a description of one.
