Delete Your Tome Account

App: Tome: Audiobook Player (Android)
Publisher: Chevis Interactive

Tome does not run its own separate accounts. When you sign in, Tome uses your Google account, and the only record Chevis Interactive keeps on our side is a small trial record in our Firebase database, containing your account identifier (Firebase user ID) and your trial start and first-play dates.

To delete that record — your Tome account as far as Chevis Interactive is concerned:

  1. Send an email to chevisinteractive@gmail.com from the Google account you used with Tome.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Tome account".
  3. We will delete your trial record within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

Good to know

  • Deleting your trial record does not cancel or refund a purchase. Purchases are managed entirely by Google Play.
  • Your playback progress backup lives in your own Google Drive, under your control. You can delete it any time: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Tome → Delete hidden app data. Your audiobook files are never affected.
  • Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall Tome or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • If you sign in to Tome again after deletion, a new trial record may be created.

Questions: chevisinteractive@gmail.com
