Delete Your Tome Data

App: Tome: Audiobook Player (Android)
Publisher: Chevis Interactive

You can delete data Tome holds without deleting your account. What exists, and how to delete it:

  1. Data on your device — playback positions, bookmarks, playback speed, listening statistics, and downloaded books. Delete it by uninstalling Tome, or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.
  2. Backup in your Google Drive — Tome saves a small backup of your progress, bookmarks, and statistics in a hidden app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. Delete it any time: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Tome → Delete hidden app data. Deleting the backup does not affect your audiobook files, which are yours and were never copied by Tome.
  3. Trial record held by Chevis Interactive — a Firebase record containing your account identifier and your trial start and first-play dates, used only to run Tome's free trial. To have it deleted, email chevisinteractive@gmail.com from the Google account you used with Tome and ask for your trial record to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Purchases are processed and managed by Google Play; Chevis Interactive never sees or stores your payment details and cannot delete purchase records held by Google.

Questions: chevisinteractive@gmail.com
