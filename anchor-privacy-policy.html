Anchor Privacy Policy

Publisher: Chevis Interactive
App: Anchor (Android)
Effective date: November 22, 2026
Contact: chevisinteractive@gmail.com

Anchor is a habit tracker published by Chevis Interactive. This policy explains what information Anchor handles, where that information stays, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Anchor collects no personal data.
  • Anchor requires no account and no sign-in.
  • All of your habit data is stored on your device only.
  • Anchor shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking.
  • Anchor shares nothing with any third party.
  • The one-time $4.99 unlock after the 30-day free period is processed entirely by Google Play, so no payment data ever reaches Chevis Interactive.

Information stored by the app on your device

When you use Anchor, the app stores the information needed to provide its features, which may include:

  • habit names, icons, colors, schedules, and goal amounts you create;
  • check-ins, streaks, best streaks, and completion history;
  • per-habit reminders and snooze settings;
  • awards you have earned;
  • theme and sound preferences;
  • home-screen widget state; and
  • trial and unlock status.

This information is stored in Anchor's app-private local storage on your device. It is never uploaded to Chevis Interactive or to anyone else.

What Chevis Interactive receives

Chevis Interactive does not receive your habits, check-ins, streaks, awards, reminders, or preferences from the app.

Anchor contains no Chevis analytics service and no advertising SDK. Google Play may provide developers with aggregate app-install, store, crash, and purchase reporting through Play Console under Google's own terms and privacy policy. That reporting is not a feed of your Anchor records.

Purchases

Anchor is free for the first 30 days. After that, a one-time $4.99 unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells Anchor whether the unlock is owned so the app can enable it.

Chevis Interactive does not receive or store your payment-card details. Google's handling of payment and Play account information is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Sharing you control

Anchor does not automatically share your records.

The app lets you share an earned award through Android's share sheet ("Share this win"). If you choose to share one, the award image or text goes only to the person or service you pick. You control whether to share it, where to send it, and whether to delete it.

Android device backup

Anchor permits Android app backup. If you enable Android's device or app backup on your device, some Anchor local data may be included in that backup under your Google account and device settings. Chevis Interactive does not operate or receive that backup.

You can manage Android backup through your device and Google account settings. Clearing Anchor's app storage or uninstalling the app removes the copy stored by the app on that device, subject to any separate backup copy you control.

Permissions

Anchor may request or use:

  • Notifications, if you allow them, for habit reminders and snooze nudges; and
  • Boot completed, so local reminders can be restored after the device restarts.

Anchor does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, call logs, text messages, or health data.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your records remain in Anchor until you edit or delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Because Chevis Interactive does not hold a server copy of your Anchor records, Chevis Interactive cannot retrieve or remotely delete the records stored on your device. Anything you chose to share remains wherever you sent it and can be deleted there.

Security

Anchor stores its working data in app-private storage provided by Android. The app does not transmit your habit records to Chevis Interactive. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep your Android backup secure, because those records describe your daily routines.

Children

Anchor is a general-purpose habit tracker and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes to how Anchor handles records will be reflected in the app and its Play Store disclosures.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Anchor's data practices: chevisinteractive@gmail.com (Chevis Interactive).
